تطريز

Tatreez, 3,000 years in the making

Tatreez is an embroidery technique that goes back to the Canaanites. For centuries Palestinians have stitched it in colours and patterns that speak of community and standing. It records our history, and it keeps our culture alive.

We are Palestinian sisters with one dream: to keep our culture and identity alive.

We do it to remember the patience and strength of our people, and to bring communities together. Wear your thobe with love and pride.

All shipping, returns and size help
Do you ship to my country?

Yes. We ship worldwide with DHL or PostNL for an extra fee, which you see at checkout. Import taxes and duties in your country are not included in our prices.

How fast do you ship?

In-stock orders ship within 4 working days. You get a track and trace code by email. Pre-order pieces ship on the date shown on the product.

Which size do I take?

Every thobe comes in sizes 1 to 4. The size guide on each product shows which size matches XS to XXL, because it differs per style.

Can I return my order?

Yes, within 14 days of delivery: unworn, unwashed and with the original tags. Return shipping is at your own cost. Sale items and pre-orders can't be returned, and an order can't be changed once it is placed.

Know when new thobes arrive

We email you when a new collection or pre-order opens.
